Vendora design system

A Windows 11 native look: Mica, layers, 4 px controls, 8 px cards and one accent. Every value comes from design/tokens.css. The theme follows the OS; the button above forces one for review.

Colour

Accent blue means act or selected. Green, amber and red are status only, always with an icon or a word.

Typography

Segoe UI Variable, with tabular figures for numbers. Cascadia Mono is only for barcodes and codes. Nothing Latin is bundled.

Motion

Fluent decelerate, no scale on press. Dialogs and menus enter from 105% to 100%. Reduced motion drops the durations to zero.

Buttons

All variants use a 4 px radius, 14 px semibold text and the double focus ring. Sizes are 36, 44 and 52 px.

Input and select

A bottom stroke in input-border. On focus the stroke becomes a 2 px accent underline, with no outer ring.

Dialog and menu

ContentDialog has an 8 px radius, the dialog shadow and a footer band with equal-width buttons. The flyout uses the flyout shadow, and its highlighted item shows the selection pill.
