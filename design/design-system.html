Bill fast, know your stock — even when the light goes.

The Vendora design system: Bazaar. Cream paper, deep emerald for the next action, saffron for what's set aside. Roomy rows, soft corners, warm shadows, and motion that answers the hand.

Personality
  • Warm, not cute: cream and emerald, never candy colours.
  • Calm, not dull: colour only when it means something.
  • Fast, not flashy: instant press feedback, motion that settles without bounce.
  • Local, not foreign: plain local words like Rs, JazzCash and Urdu names.

Token system

All values live in design/tokens.css. Names are built from the same parts, so you can guess them without looking them up, and every fill has a matching readable text colour.

Anatomy
--color bg-fill critical hover

category · property (bg, bg-surface, bg-fill, bg-tint, bg-material, text, icon, border) · role (brand, secondary, success, warning, critical, inverse, highlight) · state (hover, active, selected, disabled)

Pairs & scales
  • bg-fill-brand → text-on-brand; bg-fill-highlight → text-on-highlight; bg-surface-inverse → text-on-inverse + text-on-inverse-secondary; bg-tint-N → text-tint-N.
  • Scales use one rule: number ÷ 100 × 4 px. space-400 = 16 px, font-size-1400 = 56 px.
  • Components use only semantic tokens, never raw hex values. A test checks every documented pair for WCAG AA in light and dark.

Colour

Cream and sand build the structure. Emerald means "act or selected": the main button, the focus ring, the selected tile. Saffron means "set aside": held bills and notes, never a status. Green, amber and red carry status only, always with an icon or a word. Light is the default; dark ("Bazaar night") is a full theme.

Money & numbers
  • Rs before the amount with a space, commas for thousands: Rs 1,250,000.
  • Whole rupees by default; paisa only when an amount has them: Rs 727.63.
  • Refunds and discounts: minus sign plus critical colour, e.g. −Rs 80.
  • Weights to 3 decimals with unit (1.250 kg); pieces whole (12 pcs). Numbers right-aligned in tables.
  • Dates: 27 Oct 2026, 4:05 pm.

Depth & materials

In light, warm shadows carry a card's edge, so cards have no border. In dark, cards keep a hairline. Floating chrome (sticky table headers, the held-bills sheet) uses a translucent material that blurs what's behind it. With "reduce transparency" or "increase contrast", the material becomes the solid surface.

Motion

Motion answers the hand. Press feedback starts on pointer-down and is instant. Things enter and leave on a critically damped spring, so they settle without bounce, and a dialog grows from the control that opened it. With "reduce motion", only fades remain.

Press feedback · scale-press 0.97 · 100 ms down, 220 ms release
ease-spring · damping 1, response 0.35 s · duration-300 (350 ms)
Try it
  • Dialogs scale from scale-enter (0.96) and fade, with the origin at the trigger.
  • Reduced motion sets both scale tokens to 1 and keeps the fades.
  • Gesture springs (held-bills sheet) hand off the release velocity; parameters are in the brand kit.

Feedback

Say what happened and what to do next. A failure that doesn't stop the sale is a warning, not an error. Info stays neutral: it isn't a status.

Overlays & PIN

Dialogs are for decisions that need full attention. The manager PIN prompt opens over the bill, so the cashier never logs out to get approval. Login, lock and quick switch share the split layout: the inverse panel on the left, user tiles, PIN dots and pad on the right.

Rules
  • One dialog at a time. Esc cancels; Enter confirms the primary action.
  • Focus moves into the dialog on open and back to the trigger (or the scan field) on close.
  • Destructive confirms name the thing: "Void sale T1-000231 (Rs 4,770)?", not "Are you sure?".
  • A selected tile has a heavier emerald border and a check, not just a tint.

Void sale T1-000231?

This cancels Rs 4,770 and returns 3 items to stock. A credit note will print. This can't be undone.

Principles

1. The next scan is sacred.

Focus returns to the scan field after every action. Nothing steals focus without a visible reason.

2. Emerald means act.

The main action, focus and selection are emerald, and nothing else is. Saffron sets things aside; it never signals status.

3. Numbers are the content.

Monospaced, right-aligned, and the total is the largest thing on screen, readable at arm's length.

4. Keyboard first, touch friendly.

Every main action shows its key. Every target is at least 44 px.

5. Failure degrades, never blocks.

Printer, network or backup problems show as warnings. The sale always completes.

6. Never colour alone.

Status pairs colour with an icon and a word. Selection pairs tint with a bar, a heavier border or a check.

7. Motion answers the hand.

Press feedback is instant, motion settles without bounce, and reduced motion leaves only fades.
