Bill fast, know your stock — even when the light goes.

The Vendora design system: Mono Pro. Black, white and one indigo accent. The interface stays quiet so prices, totals and the next action stand out.

Personality
  • Precise — numbers line up, every value is exact.
  • Quiet — greys do the structure; colour only when it means something.
  • Fast — every pixel serves the next scan.
  • Premium, not cold — plain local words: Rs, JazzCash, Urdu names.

Token system

All values live in design/tokens.css. Names are built from the same parts, so they can be guessed without looking them up — and every fill has a matching readable text colour.

Anatomy
--color bg-fill critical hover

category · property (bg, bg-surface, bg-fill, text, icon, border) · role (brand, secondary, success, warning, critical, inverse) · state (hover, active, selected, disabled)

Pairs & scales
  • bg-fill-brand → text-on-brand; bg-fill-critical → text-on-critical; bg-surface-inverse → text-on-inverse.
  • Scales use one rule: number ÷ 100 × 4 px. space-400 = 16 px, font-size-1400 = 56 px.
  • Components use only semantic tokens — never raw hex values.

Colour

Zinc greys build the structure. Indigo means "act or selected" — the main button, focus ring, selected row. Green, amber and red only carry status, always with an icon or word. Light is the default; dark is a full theme.

Money & numbers
  • Rs before the amount with a space, commas for thousands: Rs 1,250,000.
  • Whole rupees by default; paisa only when an amount has them: Rs 727.63.
  • Refunds and discounts: minus sign plus critical colour — −Rs 80.
  • Weights to 3 decimals with unit (1.250 kg); pieces whole (12 pcs). Numbers right-aligned in tables.
  • Dates: 27 Oct 2026, 4:05 pm.

Feedback

Say what happened and what to do next. A failure that doesn't stop the sale is a warning, not an error. Info stays neutral grey — it isn't a status.

Overlays

Dialogs are for decisions that need full attention. The manager PIN prompt opens over the bill, so the cashier never logs out to get approval.

Rules
  • One dialog at a time. Esc cancels; Enter confirms the primary action.
  • Focus moves into the dialog on open and back to the scan field on close.
  • Destructive confirms name the thing: "Void sale T1-000231 (Rs 4,770)?" — not "Are you sure?".
  • Nothing opens mid-scan without a reason the cashier can see.

Void sale T1-000231?

This cancels Rs 4,770 and returns 3 items to stock. A credit note will print. This can't be undone.

Principles

1. The next scan is sacred.

Focus returns to the scan field after every action. Nothing steals focus without a visible reason.

2. Indigo means act.

The main action, focus and selection are indigo — and nothing else is. Status colours never look like buttons.

3. Numbers are the content.

Monospaced, right-aligned, and the total is the largest thing on screen — readable at arm's length.

4. Keyboard first, touch friendly.

Every main action shows its key. Every target is at least 44 px.

5. Failure degrades, never blocks.

Printer, network or backup problems show as warnings. The sale always completes.

6. Never colour alone.

Status pairs colour with an icon and a word; selection pairs tint with a bar.
